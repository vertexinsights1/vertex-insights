FAQs

Before we talk.

Do I need to own Power BI?

Not necessarily. We pick the simplest tool that fits your team, often Excel, and explain the options and costs before you commit.

Can you work with the data we already have?

Usually, yes. If it can be exported from your accounting software, spreadsheets or other systems, we can work with it. We tell you early if data quality is a problem.

How is my data handled?

As confidential. How it is stored, used and returned is agreed in writing before work starts.

How are fees set?

In writing, before work starts, based on the scope of the project or the monthly support you need.

What does a first project look like?

A short call, a written scope, the build with progress shared as we go, and a handover with files, a walkthrough and notes on how every number is calculated.
