Common questions.

Do I need to own Power BI?

Not necessarily. We recommend the simplest tool that fits, which is often Excel. We’ll explain the options and costs before you commit.

Can you work with the data we already have?

Usually, yes. If it can be exported from your accounting software, spreadsheets or other systems, we can work with it. We’ll tell you early if data quality is a problem.

How is my data handled?

We treat client data as confidential and agree how it is handled in writing before work starts.

How are fees set?

Fees are agreed in writing before work starts, based on the scope of the project or the monthly support you need.
