Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Vertex Insights.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect the information you enter: your name, business email, organisation, the service you are interested in, and your message.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to prepare a proposal. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

How it is delivered and stored

Form submissions are sent to a server function on our website host and stored in a private database operated by our database provider (Supabase). If email alerts are switched on, a copy is also sent to us through an email delivery provider (Resend). Only Vertex Insights staff can read enquiries. They are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting work.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics. If that changes, we will update this page and ask for consent where required.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by sending a message through the contact form.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your details: info@vertexinsights.co.in.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
