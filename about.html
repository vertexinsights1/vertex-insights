Analysis you can act on.

We help growing businesses use the data they already hold to decide with confidence.

The data exists. Nobody can use it.

Vertex Insights is an independent analytics practice. You work directly with the person doing the work.

Most growing companies already hold what they need. It sits in accounting software, spreadsheets and operating records, and never reaches the decision-maker in a usable form.

We close that gap with clear reporting, financial models and research, then leave you able to run them yourself.

Four rules we work by.

1

Start with the decision

We begin with what you need to decide, then work out which data is needed. Not the other way round.

2

Show our working

Every number can be traced back to its source, and every calculation is written down.

3

Say what the data can’t tell you

We state our assumptions and the limits of the analysis, so you know how far to rely on it.

4

Leave you capable

You keep the files, the documentation and a walkthrough, so you don’t depend on us to read your own numbers.

What we won’t do.

We don’t promise specific financial results. Analysis informs decisions, but outcomes depend on what you do with it.

We don’t publish client names or results without permission, and we don’t present sample work as real client work. Anything illustrative on this site is labelled.

We don’t sell tools or take commissions from software vendors, so our advice on what to use is not influenced by them.

Who we start with.

We start with small and medium-sized businesses in India and abroad, where one clear dashboard or model can change how the owner runs the month.

As the practice grows, we expect to take on larger organisations and to turn repeat work into standard methods. We’ll add services only when we can deliver them properly.
